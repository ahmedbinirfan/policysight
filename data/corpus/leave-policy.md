# Leave Policy — Acme MidMarket Pty Ltd

## Annual leave

Full-time staff receive **20 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year, accruing monthly. Part-time staff accrue pro-rata based on contracted hours.

## Approval

Leave requests over 5 consecutive days require manager approval at least **14 days** in advance. Same-week leave may be refused if coverage is insufficient.

## Carry-over

Unused annual leave may carry over a maximum of **5 days** into the next calendar year. Excess days are forfeited unless a written exception is approved by HR.
