# Contractor Handbook — Acme MidMarket

## Access

Contractors receive badge access to floors listed on their statement of work. After-hours access requires security escort.

## Devices

Personal laptops may be used only with MDM enrollment. USB storage of company data is prohibited.

## Escalation

Security incidents must be reported to security@acme.example within **1 hour** of discovery.
